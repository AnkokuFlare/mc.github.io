Now Kick it up to 11 with Pokemon Features!

Rebirth of an Old World

With a good deal of trial and error, The foundational island of CC2C from the BCG Pack was transfered over to a new world. With a handful for returning mods, the structures and lands got a fresh coat of new blocks and biomes. Unfortunately the was some data that will not be making the cut. Mainly do to the change of Fabric to Neoforge. Tara had mention sinytra connector for some of the missing Minecraft mods, but it leaks and crashes too frequently with Mayview's Biome Mods. Overall the world seems to runs stable from the multiple boots and exploration around the island. It's still a fixer upper, but a good deal of the foundations are in tact.

Gotta Catch 'em All

Tools like the pokenav, cobblemon emi, wondertrade station, and customizable cobblemon spawn alerts will help out with the kinds of pokemon collections your desire. Shiny odds are set to 1/1024 as a base.

Make Money or Break Money

The economy is flexible enough. Universal shops unfortunately did not make the cut, but Spud's shops filled in for a more decorative approach to custom shops.

Become the Pokemon Master

Mega showdown x All the Mons, Radical Trainer Series, Simply TMs and Raid Den Mod work hand in hand to build various Pokémon teams and progress through the challenges provided by the Trainer Cards or End game Dens.

Style on Them

Accessories, Baubles and Cosmetic Armor enables your fashion hunting needs. With several packs enhancing either your style or gameplay. The server client comes with Ears mod, for more skin customization options for horns, ears and tail.

Mob/Biome Dungeon Scaling

High risk high reward sections are available to be explored. Be sure to look out for those warning signs.
